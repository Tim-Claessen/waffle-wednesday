## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, fireside parlour digital environment built specifically for a tightly knit group of eight to nine lifelong school friends. The experience rejects the performative anxiety, notifications, and engagement loops of commercial social platforms. Instead, it evokes the cadence of a thoughtful, handwritten round-robin dispatch, the quiet hum of an evening kettle, or leaning against the kitchen bench after dinner with an old mate.

The design movement combines **warm domestic tactile minimalism** with **literary editorial grace**. The interface remains deliberately understated, prioritising deep organic dark tones suited to one-handed thumb interaction during unwinding hours. The visual atmosphere feels timeless, safe, and protected. 

There are zero algorithmic feeds, follower tallies, streak pressures, or vanity metrics. The only imagery permitted within the interface is the authentic, unpolished, three-minute video catchup recorded by a friend. UI surfaces behave like charred timber shelves, warm linen mats, and parchment labels that quietly recede so the faces and voices of lifelong companions remain central.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from natural domestic warmth: steeped black tea, hearthside embers, smoked peat, and aged paper. Dark mode serves as the primary canvas, tuned specifically for comfortable night-time reading and low-light video playback.

### Dark Mode (Default)
- **Base Canvas:** Deep espresso (`#151210`) creates a restful foundation, with soft floor steps at smoked peat (`#1C1815`) and elevated underlays (`#24201C`, `#2D2722`).
- **Surfaces & Shelves:** Warm charred timber (`#1F1B18`) and roasted walnut (`#28231F`) establish physical card bounds without harsh contrast.
- **Accents:**
  - *Primary Honeyed Amber:* `#E08B34` (interactive focus, active video states, playback scrubbing) with hover highlights in soft apricot ember (`#F2A65A`).
  - *Secondary Butter Gold:* `#F5C469` (Wednesday ritual announcements, active turn signals, gentle celebration).
  - *Tertiary Quiet Sage:* `#6E8A78` (unhurried "all caught up" statuses, low-friction checks).
- **Ink & Typography:** Soft paper cream (`#F5EFEB`) for high-contrast literary reading, muted flaxen parchment (`#D1C7BD`) for body and secondary context, and subtle hearth ash (`#8F857D`) for timestamps and metadata.
- **Dividers & Structural Ghost Borders:** Subtle warm outline `rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.08)` providing tactile edge definition without visual noise.

### Light Mode (Companion)
- **Base Canvas:** Warm clotted cream (`#FAF6F0`).
- **Surfaces:** Milled oat (`#F2ECE1`) and pressed linen (`#E8DFD1`).
- **Ink:** Deep roast espresso (`#221C18`) and steeped chicory (`#54483F`).
- **Borders:** Warm wheat hairline `rgba(34, 28, 24, 0.08)`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances literary reflection with effortless mobile legibility.

- **Newsreader** handles display headlines, weekly chapter titles, and friends' written postscripts. It provides the cadence of a novel or a printed collection of letters. It avoids harsh bold weights, relying on regular and italicised styles to communicate conversational warmth.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** grounds the functional interface: duration meters, recording controls, participant rosters, and timestamps. Its open counters and geometric humanism ensure rapid legibility at tiny scales when scanning catchups on mobile screens late at night.
- **Writing Style:** Australian English conventions (`favour`, `rumour`, `apologise`, `whilst`) are used across all standard strings, system dialogues, and empty states.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is tailored for intimate, single-thumb mobile interaction within a single viewport column, expanding gracefully to tablet and desktop whilst maintaining an uncrowded lounge feel.

### Form Factors
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stack bounded by `margin: 1.25rem`. Primary video feed behaves as a vertical story roll with `space-md` gaps. Bottom action zones maintain a permanent 48px clearance above system home indicators to ensure unhurried thumb reach for pause, reply, and next catchup.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** Centred single-track parlour frame capped at 680px maximum content width. Avoid multi-column distraction to preserve the focus of hearing from one friend at a time.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Centred ambient easel capped at 840px. The sidebar sits quietly as a soft book leaf listing the circle of friends and their latest weekly update status.

Vertical pacing uses gentle breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between friend segments, preventing the hurried claustrophobia typical of social media feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **tonal stratification** and **ambient perimeter glows**, completely eschewing hard dropshadows or artificial light sources.

1. **Surface Layers:**
   - Level 0 (Base Room): `#151210`
   - Level 1 (Shelving & Deck Tiles): `#1F1B18` enclosed with a hair-thin line `rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.08)`
   - Level 2 (Floating Response Sheets & Modal Trays): `#28231F` with a soft top rim highlight of `rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.12)`
2. **Ritual Bloom:**
   On ritual day (Wednesday), active video cards and the central record trigger receive an ambient, diffuse honey under-glow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(224, 139, 52, 0.14)`). On non-ritual days, cards rest flat with muted ash boundaries, signaling tranquility and zero urgency to reply.
3. **Glass & Translucency:**
   Floating video scrub controls and timestamp pill headers use subtle frosted glass: `rgba(28, 24, 21, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter to allow friend video colours to subtly filter through behind control surfaces.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness tier of `2` (0.5rem base corner radius), scaling up to `1rem` on cards and bottom trays, and `9999px` for tactile pills. 

This creates softened, handheld forms that feel carved from worn, hand-rubbed timber rather than machine-stamped plastic. Circular touchpads and pill-shaped badges ensure effortless fingertip alignment during low-light operation.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Touch Triggers
- **Primary Dispatch Button (Record / Send Catchup):**
  - Minimum height: 52px for generous thumb targeting.
  - Background: Honeyed Amber (`#E08B34`) with soft paper cream text (`#F5EFEB`).
  - Shape: Rounded pill (`rounded-full` / `roundedness: 3` equivalent for buttons).
  - Resting state includes a gentle amber inner glow; pressed state compresses subtly (scale `0.98`) with darkened amber tone (`#C27324`).
- **Quiet Button (Listen, Pass, Save for Later):**
  - Background: `rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.06)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.1)`.
  - Text: Flaxen Parchment (`#D1C7BD`).

### Video Catchup Cards
- **Container:** Warm charred timber background (`#1F1B18`), corner radius `rounded-lg` (1rem), bounded by hairline ghost border `rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.08)`.
- **Header Slot:** Friend's familiar name in *Newsreader* (`headline-md`), paired with casual relative timestamp (`"Yesterday afternoon"`, `"Tuesday at tea"`).
- **Video Window:** Fixed 9:16 aspect ratio with rounded inner corners (`0.75rem`). Strict absence of reaction tickers, like counts, or floating hearts. 
- **Progress Bar:** Hairline warm cream timeline (`2px`) with an amber head dot indicating elapsed time out of the three-minute limit.

### Pill Badges & Status Signals
- **Watched Indicator:** A quiet sage leaf pill (`#6E8A78` tint at 15% opacity, sage border, text `#8F857D`) that simply states *"Watched"*. Never marks someone as "delinquent" or displays overdue warnings.
- **Ritual Banner (Wednesday Glow):** A parchment banner sitting at the top of the parlour room: butter gold accent typography (`#F5C469`) with text: *"It's Wednesday. The kettle is on. 6 of 9 updates in."*

### Form Inputs & Written Notes
- **Catchup Note Field (Optional 140-char dispatch):**
  - Background: Deep espresso inset (`#151210`).
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.1)`.
  - Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans `body-md` in Soft Paper Cream (`#F5EFEB`).
  - Focus state: Outline transitions smoothly to Honeyed Amber (`#E08B34`) with no harsh ring offset.

### Modals & Audio/Video Trays
- Bottom-sheet sheets slide up smoothly using warm charred surfaces (`#28231F`), anchored by a pill-shaped drag handle (`#8F857D` at 40% opacity, 36px wide by 4px tall).
